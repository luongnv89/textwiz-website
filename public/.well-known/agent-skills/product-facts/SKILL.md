# Product facts

TextWiz is a private, local-first macOS app that runs AI "wizards" on selected
text via ⌘C + ⌘⇧Space or macOS Services — no Accessibility permission. Engines:
on-device Apple Intelligence, Ollama, LM Studio, and MLX-LM, plus cloud
providers (Gemini, OpenAI, Claude, Mistral, Groq, OpenRouter) with your own key.

The app is a free download on the Mac App Store; the Demo provider is free
forever. Real providers require TextWiz Pro: $2.99/week ($0.99 first-week intro
for eligible customers), $7.99/month, or $59.99/year US reference prices.

Authoritative sources: https://textwiz.pro/ (product), /getting-started
(setup), /privacy, /terms, and https://textwiz.pro/llms-full.txt (full context).
