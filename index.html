TextWiz — AI text shortcuts for macOS

TextWiz lets you copy text with ⌘C, press ⌘⇧Space, and run AI shortcuts from a floating panel, or use right-click → Services to replace selected text in place. Pick Apple Intelligence (on-device Foundation Model) or another engine: Gemini, OpenAI, Ollama, LM Studio, MLX-LM, and more. API keys stay in Keychain; no Accessibility permission.

TextWiz is a free download and the built-in Demo provider is free forever. Every run against a real provider requires TextWiz Pro, an auto-renewable subscription on the Mac App Store: $2.99 per week ($0.99 for the first week), $7.99 per month, or $59.99 per year in US dollars.
